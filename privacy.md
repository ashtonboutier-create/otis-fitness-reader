# Otis Fitness Reader Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

Otis Fitness Reader is a personal-use application that allows its authorized user to access fitness and wellness data from WHOOP for personal analysis, fitness insights, and daily briefings.

## Data Accessed

After explicit authorization through WHOOP OAuth, Otis Fitness Reader may access:

- Recovery information, including recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate
- Cycle and strain information
- Sleep information
- Workout and activity information

Otis Fitness Reader requests read-only access to this information and does not modify WHOOP data.

## How Data Is Used

WHOOP data accessed by Otis Fitness Reader is used solely to provide personal fitness, recovery, sleep, and training insights to the authorized user.

The data is not used for advertising and is not sold to third parties.

## Data Sharing

Otis Fitness Reader is a personal-use application. WHOOP data accessed by the application is not sold to third parties.

## Authorization and Revocation

Access to WHOOP data requires explicit authorization through WHOOP OAuth.

The user may revoke the application's access through their WHOOP account.

## Contact

For privacy questions regarding Otis Fitness Reader, contact the application owner using the contact email associated with the WHOOP developer application.
